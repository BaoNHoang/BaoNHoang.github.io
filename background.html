Background

Adaptability, curiosity, and the experiences behind my work

Da Lat, Vietnam

I was born in Da Lat, Vietnam and moved to the United States during elementary school. Growing up across two cultures taught me to adapt quickly, communicate across different perspectives, and remain persistent when learning unfamiliar systems.

My Journey

That experience shaped how I approach engineering: stay curious, break complex problems into understandable parts, and continue learning beyond formal requirements.

Computer science became the field where I could combine analytical thinking with practical creation. Today, I apply that mindset to scientific software, machine learning, backend systems, and applications intended to support real users and research.
